So. The hero stands at my door again, sword out, sunlight in his hair, and I am, forgive me, still in my dressing gown. Forty years I've kept this tower. Forty years of prophecies, torches, young men with destiny. Do you know what destiny costs in candles alone? Sit down. Not the throne, the other chair, the one that doesn't wobble. The ritual is at midnight, the eclipse is Tuesday, the demon needs feeding, and I haven't slept since the last boy with a sword. Take the crown. Take the map. Only let me please finish this warm tea first.
